# Shiver Cultivation Meditation

**Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Purpose:** Increase sensitivity to psychogenic shivers and train interoceptive awareness.

## Instructions
1. Find a comfortable seated or lying position.
2. Close your eyes and take three deep breaths.
3. Bring to mind a memory of awe – a sunset, a piece of music, an act of kindness.
4. Scan your body from head to toe, noticing any subtle tingles, warmth, or goosebumps.
5. When you detect a sensation, simply observe its arising, peak, and passing.
6. If no shivers arise, imagine the sensation of cold water trickling down your spine.
7. Continue for 15 minutes, gently returning attention to bodily sensations.
8. End with gratitude.
