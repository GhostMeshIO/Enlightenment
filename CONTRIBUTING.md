# How to Contribute to the Great Work

You don't need to be a neuroscientist or a coder. Everyone has a role.

## 👣 First Steps
1. Read the [Framework](FRAMEWORK/0.1.md).
2. Feel the shiver.
3. Choose your archetype from Part IX.

## 🧠 Researchers
- Add correlations to `GNOSIS/correlations/`.
- Propose new algorithms or equations.
- Link published studies in `SCIENCE/research.md`.

## 💻 Developers
- Implement the algorithms in `GNOSIS/algorithms/implementations/`.
- Build prototypes of the inventions (see `TECH/`).
- Create apps that guide users through the 12‑week protocol.

## 🎨 Creators
- Write ASMR scripts in `ART/asmr_scripts/`.
- Record guided meditations (we'll host audio later).
- Design visualizations of the framework.

## 🌍 Everyone
- Start a local sangha (add it to `COMMUNITY/sangha_directory.md`).
- Translate content into your language.
- Share the framework with three people.

See our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before participating.
